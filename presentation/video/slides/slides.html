DigitalHeat · Real vs. simulated

The computing process and its control are real – homes, heat and demand are simulated

Real

  • Molecular docking on a laptop with AutoDock Vina
  • Pause and resume by the same scheduler code as the annual balance
  • Checkpoint handover over TLS between two processes on the same laptop

Replayed

  • Vina scores in the home-to-pool demo, computed in an earlier run
  • Router decision of 25 May 2025 that triggers the handover

Simulated

  • Homes, tank and pool, using real 2025 Basel weather
  • NVIDIA L4 servers, their power draw and annual jobs
  • Customers, prices, sales and costs

Conclusion: the control works today – physical heat and paying demand are questions for the pilot

Our assumptions are written down, with sources or marked as estimates
